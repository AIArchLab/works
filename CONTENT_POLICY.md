# Content policy — what may never appear here

1. No client names, employer names, case numbers, or platform names inside case content.
2. No budgets or prices.
3. Nothing from the studio's private strategy layer (trade secret). Strategy-level phrasing is the ceiling.
4. The principal is referred to only as Founder.
5. Every work is labelled Sample / Concept / Illustrative / Sanitized Example; no invented results; no unmeasurable claims (zero bugs, fully integrated, guaranteed safe, etc.).
6. Facts only from sources; anything unsourced is marked as source-missing or removed before publish.
7. Show the thinking, not the recipe (sell the product, not the logic behind it).
8. English only.
