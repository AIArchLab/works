# Content policy — what may never appear here

1. No client names, employer names, case numbers, or platform names inside case content.
2. No budgets or prices.
3. Nothing from the studio's private strategy layer (trade secret). Strategy-level phrasing is the ceiling.
4. The principal is referred to only as Founder.
5. Every work is labelled Sample / Concept / Illustrative / Sanitized Example; no invented results; no unmeasurable claims (零 bug, 完全整合, 保證安全, etc.).
6. Facts only from sources; anything unsourced is marked 來源缺 or removed before publish.
7. Show the thinking, not the recipe (賣產品，不賣背後的邏輯).
8. English first, 中文摘要 in support.
